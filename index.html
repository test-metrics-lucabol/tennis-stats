7ST Zone Map

ZoneLocationExample
1Short / danger zoneS-1 → Setup error into danger zone
2Mid / neutral zoneN-2 → Neutralizer to mid court
3Deep / safe zoneN-3 → Deep reset, smart defensive shot
4Short wide angleW-4 → Short angle winner
5Short wide angleW-5 → Short angle winner (other side)
6Deep corners / weapon zoneW-6 → Deep corner winner
7Net / touch zoneW-7 → Drop shot, volley, or net finish

MarkerMeaning

Recording Priority (during match)

  1. Serve
  2. Return
  3. +1 shot
  4. Short balls given away
  5. Wrong tactical choices
  6. Good neutralizers
  7. Weapons / winners

Do not try to capture everything. Capture the useful coaching signal.

PatternAction
